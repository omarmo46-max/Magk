Magk.ai — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Magk.ai is a free, open-source Android app (GPL v3). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

1. Data we collect

The developer does not operate a server for the app and does not collect, receive, or sell your personal data. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking libraries.

2. Data stored on your device

Your conversations, settings, memory, skills, files, and API keys are stored locally on your device. API keys are kept in encrypted on-device storage. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

3. AI providers you choose

When you chat, the app sends your messages (and any files, images, or tool results you include) directly from your device to the AI provider you configured (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or a custom endpoint) using your own API key or account. That data is handled under the provider's own terms and privacy policy. Magk.ai does not proxy these requests through any server of ours.

4. Device permissions

The app may request permissions such as camera, microphone, location, contacts, calendar, notifications, and file access. They are used only for features you trigger (for example voice input, attaching a photo, or letting the agent read your calendar). You can deny or revoke any permission in Android settings, and the related features will then be unavailable.

5. Network access

Besides your AI providers, the app connects to the internet only when you ask it to (for example browsing a web page, running a command in the sandbox that uses the network, connecting an MCP server you added, or checking for updates on GitHub).

6. Crash reports

If the app crashes, a crash report is saved locally. It is never sent automatically. You can choose to share it manually, for example with the developer, through the Android share menu.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
