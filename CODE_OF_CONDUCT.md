# Code of conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

- Be respectful and constructive; assume good faith.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing others' private information.
- Keep discussion on topic and technical where possible.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who break these rules.
Report problems privately to the maintainer via Telegram [@O_P_E_2009](https://t.me/O_P_E_2009).
